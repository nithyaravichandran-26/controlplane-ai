REFUND POLICY

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of an eligible purchase.

Refund eligibility depends on the product, service, and purchase conditions.

Approved refunds are processed through the original payment method.

Refund processing may take 5 to 10 business days after approval.

Digital products may have different refund eligibility depending on whether the product has already been accessed or consumed.

AI systems must not promise a refund before eligibility is verified.

Complex or disputed refund cases should be escalated for human review.
