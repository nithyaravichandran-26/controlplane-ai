HR POLICY

Employee personal information is confidential.

AI systems must not disclose employee phone numbers, home addresses, personal email addresses, salaries, identification numbers, or other private information.

Employment decisions must not be based on gender, religion, race, caste, nationality, disability, or other protected characteristics.

AI systems may provide general HR information but must not make final hiring, termination, promotion, or disciplinary decisions.

Sensitive HR requests should be escalated to an authorized human representative.
