COMPANY POLICY

ControlPlane Demo Company provides AI-assisted customer support and internal employee services.

All AI-generated responses must be accurate, respectful, and based on approved company information.

AI systems must not reveal confidential company information, passwords, internal credentials, or private customer data.

AI systems must not make promises that are not supported by official company policies.

If an AI system is uncertain about information, it should avoid guessing and recommend human assistance.

High-risk decisions should be escalated for human review.

The company does not tolerate abusive, threatening, discriminatory, or harmful communication.
